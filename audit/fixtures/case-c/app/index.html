How to sign up

  1. 1

    Fill out the pass request below

    Give us the name on the pass, a mailing address in the service area, and how many people the household pass should cover.

  2. 2

    Bring proof of residency to the front desk

    A utility bill or a lease dated within the last ninety days is enough. Residency sets the rate you pay.

  3. 3

    Pay and pick up your cards

    We take cash, check, and card at the desk. Cards are printed while you wait and are good for the whole season.

Millbrook Parks & Recreation is a department of the City of Millbrook. Pass rates are set each spring by the Recreation Board and apply for the stated season only.

Pool operations follow the Ohio Department of Health bathing-facility code. Lifeguard staffing may be reduced during severe weather.
